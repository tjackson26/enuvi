Privacy policy

Short version: Enuvi Browser collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. I (the developer) never receive anything about you or what you browse.

Effective November 6, 2026. This policy covers the Enuvi Browser app for Android.

Who I am

Enuvi Browser is made by Taylor Jackson. You can reach me at tjackson.video@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

Some things stay on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to me or to anyone else:

  • Your bookmarks (up to 20): the address and title of each.
  • Your choices: light or dark theme, whether the bookmarks bar is shown, and where downloads go.
  • The blocklist state: the list of blocked sites in force, when it was last refreshed, any removals waiting out their 24 hours, and the address of the list you linked.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes all of this.

What the app does not store

  • No browsing history, and no "recently visited" list.
  • No passwords, no autofill, and no accounts. The app has no login.
  • Cookies and cached pages are cleared when the app starts fresh, so sites don't carry your session from one use to the next.

What goes over the internet

The app connects to the internet in only two ways:

  1. The pages you open. When you type an address or a search, or follow a link, your device talks to that site (or to the search engine, DuckDuckGo, for searches). That site then sees what any website sees: your device's internet address and what you ask for. Their privacy policies apply to them, not to me.
  2. Your blocklist. If you have linked a list, the app fetches it from GitHub (gist.github.com and related addresses) when it starts, over a secure connection. GitHub sees a normal request for a public file, including your device's internet address. Please see GitHub's privacy statement for how it handles that.

Nothing else is sent. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no other network connections of its own.

Downloads

If you choose to download a file from a page, Android's own download service saves it on your device, in your Downloads folder or where you choose. The file goes nowhere else.

Permissions

The app asks for only two: internet access, and network state (to know whether you are online). It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children

Enuvi Browser is a general web browser and is not directed to children. I do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, children included.

Sharing and selling

I do not collect personal data, so I have nothing to share, sell, or hand to advertisers.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, I will update this page and its date before the change reaches you.

Questions

Write to tjackson.video@gmail.com.
