Ask. Answer. Done.

Enuvi Browser is a small, quiet browser for quick lookups. It keeps you out of the video and social feeds that turn one question into an hour, and the list of blocked sites is kept by someone you trust, so a bad moment can't talk you out of it.

How it works. You type a question or a site. The browser opens it, you read your answer, and you close it. There are no recommendations, no history and no home page full of tiles. A friend keeps your blocklist: sites they add are blocked right away, and sites they remove stay blocked for another 24 hours.

Read how to set up your blocklist

What it won't do

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No saved history. Cookies and the browsing session are cleared when you close it.
  • No switch to turn blocking off. That is the point.
